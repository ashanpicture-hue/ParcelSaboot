# Pricing — ParcelSaboot

ParcelSaboot is a proof-collection tool for Pakistani e-commerce sellers. It records continuous, timestamped packing videos and return-opening clips that serve as evidence for return claims on marketplaces like Daraz and couriers like TCS, Leopards, and PostEx.

## Free
- Price: Rs 0/month
- Limits: 10 parcels a month, 1 packer login
- Features: Packing and return videos, claim tracker, evidence PDF, 30-day storage
- Best for: Sellers testing the tool on low volume

## Standard
- Price: Rs 1,999/month (billed monthly) | Rs 1,9990/year (billed annually, ~2 months free)
- Limits: 300 parcels a month, 3 packer logins
- Features: Everything in Free, WhatsApp deadline alerts, item checklists per product, dashboard, 180-day storage
- Best for: Growing Daraz and courier sellers
- Recommended: Yes

## Growth
- Price: Rs 4,999/month (billed monthly) | Rs 49,990/year (billed annually, ~2 months free)
- Limits: 800 parcels a month, 10 packer logins and stations
- Features: Everything in Standard, order import by CSV, 1-year storage, priority WhatsApp support
- Best for: High-volume warehouses and multi-station operations

## Common terms
- Each parcel covers one packing clip of up to 60 seconds and, if it comes back, one return clip.
- Clips are kept for the storage period of your plan, and longer while a claim is open.
- Recording pauses when you hit your monthly parcel limit; existing clips and claims stay available.
- Payment methods will be confirmed before billing starts. Prices exclude tax.
